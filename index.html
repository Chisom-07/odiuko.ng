How it works

  1. Book a consultation. Tell us about your child and what you want to improve.
  2. We find the starting point. A short check shows what your child knows and where they need help.
  3. Sessions begin. At home or online, with a plan built around your child.
  4. You stay informed. Regular updates show how your child is doing.

At home or online?

Both give your child the same lessons and the same standard of teaching. Pick whichever fits your family’s week.

Questions parents ask

Which classes do you tutor?

Our focus is Basic 1 to 6. Contact us if your child is older.

Is in-home tutoring different from online?

No. Both offer the same lessons and the same standard of teaching.

How do I book?

Use the contact page or message us on WhatsApp. We will talk through your child’s needs and agree a plan.

How will I know my child is improving?

You will receive regular updates on what was covered and how your child is doing.
